Rules

The USU Minecraft Club is a welcoming community for Aggies. These rules exist to keep it that way. They apply to the server, our Discord, and any club activity.

In-Game
01
No griefing protected areas
Don't destroy, modify, or tamper with another player's builds or items within protected areas without their permission. This includes stealing from chests, flooding builds, and any other form of intentional damage. The server will utilize plugins to help facilitate this, but please be respectful and report issues if you see them.
02
No harassment or hate speech
Treat everyone with basic respect. Harassment, hate speech, slurs, or targeted behavior toward any player will result in immediate removal from the server.
03
No disruptive behavior
Don't intentionally ruin the experience for others. This includes spamming chat, exploiting bugs to cause problems for other players, or any behavior that's clearly meant to annoy or disrupt.
Community
04
Follow USU's Student Code
All members are expected to abide by the Utah State University Student Code in all club activities, on and off the server.
05
Violations can result in removal
Repeated or serious violations of these rules may result in removal from the server, the Discord, and/or the club itself. Removal from the club requires a majority vote of the membership per the club constitution.
Report a concern
See something wrong? Please reach out to an officer directly on Discord. Any concern involving potential harm is escalated immediately.
